      How to read the patterns

      Numbers

      The ding (center) is D. The numbers follow what you play over: 1, 2, 3… are the notes of the scale or chord from low to high, on the top and the bottom. The instrument shows the same numbers.

      Hands

      Each step shows R or L. Alternate hands by default. Strike with the pad of a finger and bounce straight off; letting it rest kills the note.

      Tak & rests

      T is a tak: a light tap on the shell between notes for a percussive click. A dot is a rest. Count the steps as eighth notes.

      Progressions

      A progression plays the pattern once over each chord in turn, then starts again. The numbers follow the chord, so the same pattern moves with the harmony. A switch comes in on beat 1.

      Keyboard

      Space or P play and stop the pattern, D ding, 1–9 and 0 the numbered notes, T tak. You can play along while a pattern runs.

      Volume

      Sounds are synthesized in the browser. Tunings show common layouts; real instruments vary by maker.
